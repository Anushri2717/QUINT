Chapter one: Guessed

At first you use whatever is in the bathroom. A friend's cleanser, a free sample, the jar someone swore by. Nobody tells you what any of it does. You put it on and hope.

Chapter two: Overloaded

Then you find skincare, and it finds you. Ten-step routines. Serums with a dozen actives. Acids layered on acids. The shelf gets fuller, the skin gets angrier, and you can't tell which bottle did it.

Chapter three: Chosen

Then you work it out. Your skin doesn't want everything. It wants the right active, at the right strength, in the right amount. One thing you understand, used every day.

That's the skin
we made this for.

Five serums, one active each, with the drop count printed on the bottle. Light enough to use every day, simple enough that you always know what's working.

Quint means five. You'll rarely need all of them, and that's the point: pick the one your skin is asking for, and leave the rest on our shelf, not yours.

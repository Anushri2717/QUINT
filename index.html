One rule: Vitamin C in the morning, Retinol at night. Never both in the same routine.

Retinol starter plan

Retinol is
a slow build

Going nightly from day one is how people end up red and peeling. Start with two nights a week and add more only when your skin stays calm.

Retinol nights by week. Weeks 1 to 2: Tuesday and Friday. Weeks 3 to 4: Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Weeks 5 to 6: Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday. Week 7 onward: every night.
MTWTFSS

Filled = retinol night. Stay on a step longer if skin feels tight or flaky.
